Typography and Text Styling

Good typography makes text readable and visually appealing. You can control font families, sizes, weights, and spacing.

This text should be uppercase

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. The arrangement of type involves selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths, line-spacing, and letter-spacing.

Typography Tips

Use consistent font families throughout your site. Limit yourself to 2-3 different fonts maximum.
